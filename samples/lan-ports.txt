Note on methodology: a burst scan of all 65535 ports (400 parallel connects,
0.7 s timeout) reported nothing open, which is a false negative — the same
script confirms 36669 and 18400 as open in a single paced connect. The TV
appears to drop SYNs under scanning load, so burst results are not usable.
Only per-port paced checks are trustworthy here.
